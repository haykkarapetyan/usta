Good to know

Time

Three clocks

Yerevan runs 2h ahead of Madrid, and Portugal an hour behind Spain. Clocks go back across Europe on Sun 25 Oct — the same day as the Lisbon coach.

Emergency

112 everywhere

One number for police, fire and ambulance across Spain, Portugal and Italy, free from any phone. Madrid apartment: +34 651 97 15 79.

Money

Euro throughout

Italy, Spain and Portugal are all euro. Cards are accepted almost everywhere; small cash is handy for Porto.

Bags

Wizz cabin rules

Three Wizz bookings covering four flights, for five people — the allowance is per booking, so worth checking each one before check-in.

Airport

Two nights in T1

Every Wizz flight uses Terminal 1, so no transfers — but both Malpensa layovers are overnight, roughly 01:10–06:40 on the way out and 23:30–05:00 on the way back.
